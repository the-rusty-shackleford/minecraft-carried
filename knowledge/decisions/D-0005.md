# D-0005 — Trading pays out of the stores; what is not spent goes back where a give goes

Accepted 2026-09-28, in the proposal Rusty approved ("yes" to the trading hook).

Choosing a trade fills its payment slots from the merchant menu's own inventory slots (3–38) and
nowhere else (`MerchantMenu.moveFromInventoryToPaymentSlot`, read in bytecode). On the server a
mixin at its end tops each payment slot up from the stores with the same kind (`Carried.draw`,
stores only; the inventory's went first). Closing the screen hands the payment back with the
inventory's `placeItemBackInInventory`, which drops what does not fit; wrapped, it gives through
Carried first, so a payment drawn out of a bag goes back into it when the inventory is full rather
than onto the ground, and only a remainder takes vanilla's path. The client's own prediction of the
fill does not see the stores; the server's slot update corrects it.

Reaches vanilla villagers and wandering traders and any mod using vanilla's merchant menu. Mowzie's
Mobs' Umvuthi trades through its own menu and is not reached.

Verified: `HookGameTests`: a villager's trade paid from a store and the unspent emeralds back in an
empty slot; with the inventory full, back in the store and nothing dropped. The second failed with
the closing wrap removed (the emeralds were dropped).
