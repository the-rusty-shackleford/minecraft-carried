# D-0001 — The Carried protocol: one answer to "what does this player carry?"

Accepted 2026-09-28. Rusty, after Village Deed's purchase could not see the emeralds in his
Backpacks+ bag, the latest of several mods fixed one at a time: "we need to make a single
modification that makes all things that see your inventory also see your backpack. This game of
whack a mole is retarded." His direction: code that loops over the inventory calls one shared
helper, a protocol, which answers inventory questions for a player with their worn backpacks in
it; Backpacks+ provides the bags; every one of our mods moves onto it. He approved the proposal
(the audit is in [../design/audit.md](../design/audit.md)) and named it: "Carried is good".

## What a player carries

The inventory's main slots (the hotbar first, as the inventory numbers them, 0–35), then the
offhand (40), then every **store** the registered **providers** list, in their order, cells in
order. Armour is never carried stock (nobody pays with a helmet). A store is storage a player
carries: a backpack's storage cells, a bag in a pocket, a quiver; never gear a player holstered (a
backpack's mounts are not a store). With no provider loaded Carried is the inventory alone, which
is how every consumer behaved before.

## The protocol, `com.chunkworks.carried.api`

- **`CarriedStore`**: `address()` (unique among the player's stores now, stable while the store
  stays where it is, namespaced), `size()`, `peek(cell)` (read-only; a store may hand out a
  snapshot), `admits(cell, stack)` (the store's own rules), `write(cells)` (server only; replaces
  the contents as one change; a withdrawal is always accepted, a placement only where `admits`
  said so). A store is good for the one operation that listed it.
- **`CarriedProvider`**: `id()`, `stores(player, out)` (either side; changes nothing; allocates
  nothing on the steady state). **`CarriedProviders`**: the ordered registry, duplicates refused,
  registered from a mod's constructor, as the village and AzimuthLocation protocols keep theirs.
- **`Carried`**, reads on either side: `count` (by predicate or item), `has`, `find` (the first
  place holding a kind, in everything or the stores alone), `at` (a place's stack), `forEach` and
  `forEachStored` (every carried stack with its store and cell).
- **`Carried`**, changes on the logical server only (a client call throws): `take(player, kind, n,
  taken)` (all or nothing: planned over everything in reach, applied only when it covers `n`,
  inventory before stores, so a bag is the reserve; for payments); `draw(player, kind, max,
  storesOnly)` (up to `max` of one kind, the first match deciding it; for filling a slot);
  `takeFrom(player, store, cell, n)` (one place); `give(player, stack)` and `giveOrDrop` (the
  inventory's stacks, the stores' stacks, the inventory's empty slots, the stores' empty cells:
  Backpacks+ D-0029's order); `pickUp` (give with a caller's own inventory step, for the pickup
  hook).

The rules that decide amounts are pure and tested by JUnit in `domain`: `Placement` (the two
passes of a give; Backpacks+'s `PickupPlan`, moved), `Withdrawal` (all or nothing; up to), `Loan`
(D-0003).

## Where it lives

Its own repo and a small NeoForge mod, nested (jarJar, `strictly [1.0,2.0)`) in Backpacks+ and in
every consumer, the way the ranged-weapons protocol ships; the loader keeps one copy, the highest.
A consumer works with or without Backpacks+. The vanilla hooks live here too (D-0002 to D-0005):
they are rules about carried storage, so a new provider gets them by registering.

## Cost

HUDs count every frame and Ranged Weapons Mod's gunnery every tick, so reads allocate nothing:
providers push their stores into a per-thread reused list (a small stack of them, since a visitor
may ask Carried something), the registry is a replaced array, and counting walks the inventory's
lists and the stores' cells in place. Measured by `countingAllocatesNothing` with the JVM's
per-thread allocation counter: 150–172 ns a count over 36 slots and a 36-cell store, 0 bytes over
100,000 calls (Backpacks+'s real bag: 287 ns, 0 bytes, its D-0032). A change plans over a
snapshot of the reach and writes each store once per step.

## Rejected

- Making a bag look like extra inventory slots (a mixin on `Inventory.getContainerSize` and
  `getItem`), which every loop in every mod would see: death drops, grave mods, `/clear` and slot
  packets would then handle the bag's contents and the bag, which duplicates items.
- Extending NeoForge's player item-handler capability: in the pack only Dusty Decorations' cash
  register and NeoForge's fluid helper use it, and it addresses slots by index.
- A cache of per-item counts per store revision (the proposal's first idea): counting in place is
  already allocation-free and a fraction of a microsecond, and a cache would be one more thing to
  keep true.
- Stores listing gear: consumers would each have to know to leave it out.

## Verification

JUnit: `PlacementTest`, `WithdrawalTest`, `LoanTest` (14). GameTests with a fake provider
(`carried_gametest`): reads (order, armour never, two stores), take (all or nothing, inventory
first, one write), draw and takeFrom, give (the four steps in order; a refusal stays; giveOrDrop
drops), a pickup overflowing into a store, the cost (7); the hooks (7, D-0002 to D-0005).
