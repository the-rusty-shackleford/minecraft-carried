# D-0002 — Pickups go into the stores, placed as one inventory

Accepted 2026-09-28; carries Backpacks+ D-0029 (2026-09-27, Rusty: pickups "should stack just like
the regular inventory works") into the protocol, unchanged in effect, so any provider's stores
take pickups.

A MixinExtras `@WrapOperation` on the `Inventory.add` call in `ItemEntity.playerTouch` and in
`AbstractArrow.tryPickup` runs `Carried.pickUp`: the inventory's stacks of the same thing, the
stores' stacks, the inventory's own add (the wrapped call, so another mod's wrap of it still runs,
and creative's discard with it), the stores' empty cells. A wrap, not a redirect. On a client, for
an empty stack or with no store carried, it is exactly the wrapped call.

Rejected (as in D-0029): wrapping `Inventory.add` itself, which would send `/give`, crafting results
and every other mod's insertion into the stores.

Verified: `CarriedGameTests.aPickupOverflowsIntoAStore` here; Backpacks+'s eleven pickup GameTests
and its Curios one, against real bags through this code.
