# D-0004 — The recipe book counts and fills from the stores

Accepted 2026-09-28, in the proposal Rusty approved ("yes" to the recipe-book hook).

Vanilla's recipe book asks the inventory for its stacks in one place, `Inventory.fillStackedContents`,
on the client (which recipes it shows craftable) and on the server (whether it can place one), and
its server fill, `ServerPlaceRecipe.moveItemToGrid`, moves one stack at a time from an inventory slot
it finds and gives up when it finds none. Two mixins: the stores' simple stacks join the count
(`accountSimpleStack`, as vanilla counts the inventory's main slots); and before the fill gives up it
takes, with `Carried.draw` from the stores, the kind vanilla would have accepted (the same item and
components, undamaged, unenchanted, unnamed). The inventory is still first. The two ship together:
counting without taking would show a recipe craftable and then place half of it.

This reaches the book at the inventory and the crafting table (both tested), the three furnaces
(the same placement code, `RecipeBookMenu.handlePlacement`; not tested), Craftlight (its crafting
runs through vanilla's placement; its GameTest makes planks from a bag's logs, Craftlight unchanged) and Warehouse Manager's pooled fill (it counts through
the same method). No other mod in the pack mixes into either method (bytecode scan of the pack's
jars).

Expected from vanilla's code, not observed: the book refreshes its count on the client when the
inventory's change counter moves, which a bag in a Curios slot changing alone does not do; the count
would catch up the next time the book is opened or the inventory changes.

Verified: `HookGameTests`: the book counts a store's logs and places planks from them, one and then
all (shift); the inventory's log first. Backpacks+ `CraftingTableGameTests`: a stone pickaxe placed at
a real table from sticks and cobblestone only in the worn bag. On Backpacks+'s fixture the book at the
table outlines as craftable the cobblestone recipes whose only cobblestone is in the bag.
