# D-0003 — Projectiles in a store count for the weapon in hand; the store pays for what the copy lost

Accepted 2026-09-28; carries Backpacks+ D-0026 (2026-09-24, Rusty: "My bow shows as empty even
though I have many arrows in my backpack") into the protocol, with its debit corrected.

After vanilla has looked in the hands and the inventory and found nothing, NeoForge's
`LivingGetProjectileEvent` lets a listener substitute a stack; Carried lends a copy of the first
stored stack the weapon accepts, marked under custom data (`carried_lent`: the store and cell), since
a store's cells are read-only. The inventory's projectiles still come first.

**The debit.** Backpacks+ took the arrow out of the bag only inside vanilla's
`ProjectileWeaponItem.useAmmo`. The audit found a weapon that never calls it: Create's potato cannon
asks `Player.getProjectile` for a potato and then shrinks the stack it was handed itself
(`ammo.shrink(1)` in `PotatoCannonItem.use`, read in its bytecode), so the bag lent a copy, the copy
shrank and the bag kept its potato: free ammunition. Now a loan is recorded (server only, by the
copy's identity) and settled by what the copy lost, `Loan.debit` (never more than the cell still
holds): at `useAmmo`'s return, so a crossbow's cell is one short the moment it is loaded, and again
at the end of the server tick, which catches any weapon that shrinks the copy itself; a lookup a
weapon never used settles to nothing, and so does Infinity or creative (vanilla's `useAmmo` leaves
the copy whole then). The marker is dropped from the copy and from what the weapon took, so the
arrow that flies stacks with plain arrows; Backpacks+ 0.5's key is dropped too, should a marked
stack survive in a world.

Rejected: a hook on Create's cannon (the whack-a-mole this protocol ends); lending the store's own
stack (a store's cells are read-only; Backpacks+ D-0004).

Verified: `HookGameTests`: a bow shot paid at once and not again at the end of the tick, the arrow
unmarked; `aWeaponThatShrinksTheCopyItselfIsPaidAtTheEndOfTheTick` with a test weapon that does as
the potato cannon does, which failed on the old rule (the store kept all 8 arrows after two shots);
Infinity pays nothing; the inventory's arrows come first. Backpacks+'s four ammo GameTests against
real bags. Not run with Create itself.
